The numbers behind ads

Ads are maths before they are magic

Most beginners spend money without knowing what a sale should cost them. Here is the kind of simple calculation you will learn to do before you spend:

Your product price₦5,000
Ad budget for the test₦20,000
Buyers the ad brings6
Cost to get one buyer₦3,333
Sales minus ad spend₦10,000

Example numbers for illustration only, not a result you should expect. Your product, price and audience will give different numbers. The point is knowing them before you scale.

In the live training you will learn what these numbers mean, which ones to watch, and how to decide whether an ad is worth keeping.
